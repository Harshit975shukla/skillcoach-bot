From request to your first lesson

  1. 1. Request and approval

    The owner reviews your request. Repeating it does not move you ahead or send another alert.

  2. 2. A short, private setup

    Share your goal, experience and available time. A resume and job description are optional. Answer five diagnostic questions; not knowing yet is fine.

  3. 3. Review your study week

    See topics, objectives and practice. Change the proposal or approve it, then choose when Day 1 starts. No artificial analysis wait.

Before guided setup, you decide whether to share your approved learning plan and progress summaries with the owner. Documents, private answers and conversations are excluded. Coaching uses configured AI providers; estimates are not certification or job guarantees.
